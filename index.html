Our Story

It started in 2015, with a petition. A group of Ashesi students, tired of the same cafeteria rotation, demanded shawarma — real shawarma — on campus. When no one answered, two students decided to answer it themselves.

They set up a single charcoal grill outside the SRC building, using a spice blend one of them had learned from his uncle in Kumasi. The first day, they sold out in 40 minutes. The second day, the queue stretched past the library. By the end of the semester, students were skipping lunch just to be first in line.

Ten years later, Grillos is still on the same spot, still using the same recipe, still grilled to order. We've been voted the best shawarma spot at Ashesi every year since we opened — and the queue is still worth it.
